The studio

Architecture that listens before it speaks.

I'm Lucy Mkhoyan — a licensed architect who grew up between Yerevan's pink tuff façades and the forests of Tavush. After studying at the National University of Architecture and Construction of Armenia and working in studios in Milan and Rotterdam, I opened my own practice in 2014.

The studio stays deliberately small. Every project is led personally from the first site visit to the last door handle, with a trusted circle of engineers, makers and builders. I believe good design is mostly patience: understanding the land, the light and the people before drawing a single line.

— Lucy
