Beginner · 2× a week · ~45 min

Full body, twice a week.

Two simple workouts, A and B. Alternate them with at least one rest day in between — e.g. Monday & Thursday.

The plan

    Start with a 5-minute warm-up (bike, rower or brisk walk) and one light practice set of the first exercise.

    How to progress

    1. Pick a weight you can lift for the bottom of the rep range with 2–3 reps left in the tank.
    2. Each session, try to add a rep to each set.
    3. When you hit the top of the range on every set, add a little weight next time. The app will tell you.
    4. Rest 60–90 seconds between sets. Good form beats heavy weight — always.
